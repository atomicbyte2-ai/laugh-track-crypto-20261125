# Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest

> I bought the dip. The dip bought a shovel, dug deeper, and now my portfolio lives closer to the Earth's core than to the moon.

The joke starts from the most repeated piece of trading advice — buying the dip — and lets it go wrong in the most literal way possible: the dip turns out to be an active opponent that grabs a shovel and keeps digging. The final twist flips the genre's favourite destination on its head, sending the portfolio down toward the centre of the Earth instead of up to the moon, which is exactly how a dip that keeps dipping feels to anyone who has watched a chart fall after confidently catching it.
